SIH Problem Statement SIH26033

Eliminating the Multi-Tier Middleman Drain

In India's conventional agriculture supply chain, a crop passes through 4 to 6 intermediaries before reaching the consumer. Farmers receive less than 30% of the retail price while consumers pay exorbitant markups.

❌ Traditional Mandi Chain

  • 🔴
    4-6 Layers of Intermediaries: Village aggregators, local commission agents (arhatiyas), wholesale mandis, transport brokers, and city retailers each take a cut.
  • 🔴
    Exploitative Pricing & Distress Sales: Farmers are forced to accept whatever the local cartel quotes on harvest day, often below production costs.
  • 🔴
    No Demand Visibility: Farmers plant crops blindly without market intelligence, leading to massive gluts, rotting produce, and crashing prices.
  • 🔴
    Consumer Pays +60% Extra: By the time produce reaches dining tables, retail prices are inflated and freshness is degraded by 3-5 days.

✅ The KisanConnect Direct Way

  • 🟢
    Direct Farmer-to-Buyer Contract: Zero middleman commissions. Farmers list directly at fair farm-gate prices.
  • 🟢
    ~22% Higher Farm Incomes: Farmers retain the bulk of value addition and receive transparent digital settlements.
  • 🟢
    AI-Driven Demand Forecasting: Pre-harvest predictive analytics forecast 7-14 day urban demand so farmers plant and harvest profitably.
  • 🟢
    Cold-Chain Logistics & Bulk Tiers: Direct farm-gate pickup with clear status tracking for household consumers, restaurants, and apartments.
